# PanFire System Directives & Design Rules

## Brand & Aesthetic Concept
Wood-fired pizza first, Asian small plates second. One clear identity.
Tone: Confident, blunt, warm. Like a loud neighbourhood pizzeria zine, not a tech startup.
Taste: Bold, editorial, slightly brutalist, award-site quality.

## Strict Design System
- Colours: cream #F3ECDD, char black #161412, oven red #C8371A, ash grey #8A8378. Nothing else.
- Flat colour only. NO gradients, NO glow, NO soft blur shadows, NO glassmorphism.
- Fonts:
  - Fraunces (Headlines, huge, tight tracking, occasional italic word). Hero headline: clamp(4rem, 12vw, 12rem).
  - Instrument Sans (Body, 17-18px).
  - JetBrains Mono (Prices, labels, specs).
- Borders: 1.5px solid #161412. Corners: 0-4px. Hard offset shadows: 4px 4px 0 #161412 on interactive elements.
- Asymmetric 12-column grid. Generous whitespace. Fewer boxes.

## Hard Banned
Gradients, glow, pill badges, card inside card, 3-icon stat rows, emoji,
purple/neon/orange-gradient, centered-everything layouts, stock-looking imagery,
words like "Artisanal", "Haute", "Happy Foodies", "100% Hygienic", "Elevate",
"Crafted with passion". No fake ratings, review counts, or testimonials.
No pesto drizzle on a Margherita, no fake toggles that do nothing.
